01 About

I'm Sanjeeva — an engineer who likes the unglamorous middle of a project: the data model, the edge case, the interface that disappears once it's right.

Most of what I build starts as a personal itch — a spreadsheet that got out of hand, a routine that deserved automating, a workflow a friend complained about once too often. A few of those experiments outgrew my own use and turned into the projects below.

Outside of writing code, I'm usually reading about type systems, re-tuning a mechanical keyboard, or arguing that tabs and spaces both have their place.

02 Selected Work
01

Tidewatch

A coastal weather dashboard built for small-boat fishers along the southern coast — swell height, wind shifts, and tide tables in one glance, without the ad clutter of marine forecast sites.

  • Svelte
  • Go
  • NOAA/weather APIs
02

Ledgerline

An expense-splitting tool for shared households that handles the messy cases spreadsheets don't — uneven splits, recurring bills, and the one roommate who always forgets to log the electricity payment.

  • TypeScript
  • Postgres
  • PWA
03

Quietroom

A focus-timer with ambient soundscapes, built after one too many overlong "just checking one thing" browser sessions. No streaks, no leaderboards — just a timer and a good field-recording of rain.

  • React
  • Web Audio API
